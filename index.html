Plan questions
What does Framewright actually do with a video?

It runs a full post pass: a word-by-word transcript with speaker turns, scene and loudness maps, ranked clip candidates, caption styles and chapter notes — then lets you cut the footage by editing the text.

Which links can I hand it?

Public links from the major video and social platforms, direct media URLs, and recordings from meeting tools — if a browser can play it, the workbench can usually pull it in.

What files can I drop?

MP4, MOV and WebM video, plus MP3 and WAV audio for transcript-only jobs. Drag them onto the page — uploads resume if your connection hiccups mid-flight.

How many languages are covered?

120+ for transcription, captions and translation, with speaker labels and click-to-seek timestamps on every line.

Is there a real free way in?

Yes — the free bench includes the editor, the cut room, transcripts and captions, with unbranded exports and no card asked for at any point.

Do manual edits spend credits?

No. Trimming, text overlays, restyling and re-exporting are free forever; only the heavy AI passes bill credits — and those are unmetered while the studio is in beta.
