Product Q&A
Can I try Framewright for free?

Yes. Sign up and 100 credits land in your account straight away — no card, no trial clock.

Can I download invoices and receipts?

Yes. Invoices and receipts download as PDFs from Settings > Plan > Invoice inside the web app.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

Yes. Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew on the billing date unless you cancel before it.

Which payment methods do you support?

Major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link and a few more at checkout.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Settings > Profile > Current Plan, then “Change”. Benefits run to the end of the billing cycle and expire on their own after that. Payments are nonrefundable.

What happens to unused monthly credits?

Monthly credits reset at the end of each billing cycle. Unused ones don’t roll over.

How do annual credits work?

On an annual plan the full allowance arrives upfront. Each new annual cycle resets the balance to that year’s allowance; leftovers from the previous year expire.

Where can I learn how credits work?

The Help Center covers it. Clipping and AI Notes generally cost 1 credit per input minute; Find Moments after a clipping job costs 0.5 credits per minute.

Is there a community I can join?

Yes — our Discord is open for feature requests, workflows and clip showcases alike.
